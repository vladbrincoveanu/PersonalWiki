## Summary
Monish discusses Charlie Munger's psychology of human misjudgment, emphasizing that mental models are truths that initially surprise us but, when taken seriously and cascaded together, create powerful Lollapalooza effects. He introduces David Hawkins' concept that truth operates on a log scale rather than binary, where moving toward extreme truth leads to exponential trust and payoff, illustrated through Costco's business practices.

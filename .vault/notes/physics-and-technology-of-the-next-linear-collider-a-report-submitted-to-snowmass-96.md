## Summary
This 1996 report presents design expectations and physics programs for an e+e- linear collider operating at center-of-mass energies of 500 GeV to 1 TeV. It reviews proposed experiments and demonstrates the machine's potential for exploring physics beyond the Standard Model. The report also establishes feasibility through an assessment of current linear collider technology and presents the 'zeroth-order' design specifications.

## Key Facts
- The proposed e+e- linear collider targets center-of-mass energies between 500 GeV and 1 TeV
- The report was jointly prepared by the NLC ZDR Design Group and NLC Physics Working Groups
- Document spans 207 pages with 98 figures, covering both design specifications and physics research programs

> Abstract: We present the current expectations for the design and physics program of an e+e- linear collider of center of mass energy 500 GeV -- 1 TeV. We review the experiments that would be carried out at this facility and demonstrate its key role in exploring physics beyond the Standard Model over the full range of theoretical possibilities. We then show the feasibility of constructing this machine, by reviewing the current status of linear collider technology and by presenting a precis of our `zeroth-order' design.

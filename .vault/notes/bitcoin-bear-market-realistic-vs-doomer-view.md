## Summary
This analysis compares realistic versus doomer perspectives on Bitcoin's current bear market cycle. The realistic view projects a 70% drop from highs (to ~$30-42k), consistent with prior bear market cycles of 77-94%. The presenter argues this level of decline is historically normal and not 'doomer' thinking, especially given late-cycle economic conditions and expected US recession signals.

## Key Facts
- Bitcoin topped at ~$126,000 in Q4 2025, entering a bear market expected to last around one year
- Prior Bitcoin bear markets saw drops of 94%, 87%, 84%, and 77% respectively
- A 65% drop from highs targets ~$42,000; 75% drop targets ~$30-31,000
- Q4 2025 US real GDP came in at 0.5%, signaling economic weakness
- Year-over-year employment growth is nearly negative, historically correlating with recessions
- 2019 showed Bitcoin can drop 70% even from a non-euphoric top during recession
- Business cycle当前位置 resembles 2019, 2007, 2000, 1989, 1980, 1974, and 1969
- Base case expects market cycle bottom several months away, potentially later than May

## Summary
This 1997 paper by Alexander L. Bondarev presents two computational approaches for reducing mathematical complexity in high energy physics calculations: covariant methods for computing amplitudes with polarized Dirac particles, and algebraic reduction techniques for simplifying traces involving ten or more Dirac gamma-matrices.

## Key Findings
- The paper was presented at the XVIII Workshop on High Energy Physics and Field Theory in Protvino, Russia (June 1995)
- Two distinct minimization approaches are covered: covariant amplitude calculations for polarized particles and trace reduction for multiple gamma-matrices
- The author is affiliated with NC PHEP (National Center for Particle and High Energy Physics) in Minsk, Belarus
- The work addresses practical computational bottlenecks common in high energy physics phenomenology calculations

> Abstract: Two approaches to calculations' minimization in High Energy Physics are considered. The first one is the method of covariant calculations for the amplitudes of processes with polarized Dirac particles. The second one connects with the possibility to reduce the expressions for the traces of products of ten and more Dirac gamma-matrices.

## Summary
A concise 3-page document presenting key questions about the top quark posed by Chris Quigg of Fermilab for discussion at the 1996 DPF/DPB Summer Study on New Directions for High-Energy Physics (Snowmass 96). The paper represents a summary of research priorities and open questions in top quark physics as understood just after the top quark's discovery.

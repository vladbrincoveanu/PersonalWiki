## Summary
This paper presents an exact analytical solution of the Schrodinger equation for the Mie potential across arbitrary angular momentum states. The Nikiforov-Uvarov method is employed to calculate energy eigenvalues and corresponding wavefunctions expressed through Jacobi polynomials. Numerical results for bound states are provided for specific angular momentum values.

## Key Facts
- Exact solution obtained for Schrodinger equation with Mie potential for arbitrary angular momentum
- Nikiforov-Uvarov method used to derive energy eigenvalues and wavefunctions
- Wavefunctions expressed in terms of Jacobi polynomials
- Published in Journal of Mathematical Chemistry, Vol. 43, 749 (2007)

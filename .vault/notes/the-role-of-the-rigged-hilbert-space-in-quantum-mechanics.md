## Summary
This paper argues that rigged Hilbert space, not Hilbert space alone, provides the natural mathematical framework for quantum mechanics when continuous spectrum is present. Through a simple exactly solvable example, the author demonstrates how Dirac's bra-ket formalism is fully implemented within rigged Hilbert space, offering both a constructive procedure and physical significance discussion.

## Key Facts
- Rigged Hilbert space extends Hilbert space to properly handle continuous spectrum in quantum mechanics
- Dirac's bra-ket formalism is fully implemented by rigged Hilbert space rather than Hilbert space alone
- Paper uses a simple exactly solvable example as pedagogical tool
- Published in European Journal of Physics, vol 26 (2005), pages 287-312
- Contains 29 pages and 2 figures

#  Title:The role of the rigged Hilbert space in Quantum Mechanics
Authors:[R. de la Madrid](https://arxiv.org/search/quant-ph?searchtype=author&query=de+la+Madrid,+R)

[View PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0502053v1)
> Abstract: There is compelling evidence that, when continuous spectrum is present, the natural mathematical setting for Quantum Mechanics is the rigged Hilbert space rather than just the Hilbert space. In particular, Dirac's bra-ket formalism is fully implemented by the rigged Hilbert space rather than just by the Hilbert space. In this paper, we provide a pedestrian introduction to the role the rigged Hilbert space plays in Quantum Mechanics, by way of a simple, exactly solvable example. The procedure will be constructive and based on a recent publication. We also provide a thorough discussion on the physical significance of the rigged Hilbert space.

## Summary
Paul Langacker provides a comprehensive 22-page overview of the Standard Model covering its Lagrangian structure, spontaneous symmetry breaking, mass eigenstates, and gauge interactions, along with discussion of the model's inherent problems and limitations.

## Key Facts
- Originally published in 1995 in Precision Tests of the Standard Electroweak Model, submitted to arXiv in 2003
- Covers the Standard Model Lagrangian and its reexpression in terms of mass eigenstates after symmetry breaking
- Describes gauge interactions and key problems facing the Standard Model
- Contains 6 figures and is cited in high-energy physics literature

> Abstract: The structure of the standard model is concisely summarized, including the standard model Lagrangian, spontaneous symmetry breaking, the reexpression of the Lagrangian in terms of mass eigenstates after symmetry breaking, and the gauge interactions. The problems of the standard model are described.

## Summary
This paper establishes a converse of the celebrated Jensen inequality for differentiable convex mappings of several variables. The author applies this converse result to derive counterpart versions of well-known analytic inequalities. Additionally, applications to Shannon's and Renyi's entropy mappings are presented.

## Key Facts
- Presents converse of Jensen inequality for convex mappings of several variables
- Classified under MSC 26D15 (Inequalities) and 94A05 (Communication, information theory)
- Authored by Sever Silvestru Dragomir, submitted April 2003

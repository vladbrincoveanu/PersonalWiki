## Summary
This paper investigates probability distributions for the cosmological constant in the Arkani-Hamed-Dimopoulos-Kachru (ADK) string theory landscape. Building on the Bousso-Polchinski discretuum model, the author finds that the cosmological constant probability distribution varies wildly, challenging anthropic predictions which depend crucially on assuming a flat prior distribution. The work conjectures that this staggered distribution pattern would arise in any landscape model generating a dense spectrum of low-energy constants.

## Key Facts
- The Bousso-Polchinski discretuum model produces wildly varying cosmological constant probability distributions
- Successful anthropic predictions of the cosmological constant require assumption of a flat prior distribution
- The staggered character of probability distributions likely appears in any landscape model with dense low-energy constant spectra
- The paper calculates the volume distribution for the cosmological constant (Λ)

> Abstract: In a previous paper we found that in the context of the string theory ``discretuum'' proposed by Bousso and Polchinski, the cosmological constant probability distribution varies wildly. However, the successful anthropic predictions of the cosmological constant depend crucially on the assumption of a flat prior distribution. We conjectured that the staggered character of our Bousso-Polchinski distribution will arise in any landscape model which generates a dense spectrum of low-energy constants from a wide distribution of states in the parameter space of the fundamental theory. Here we calculate the volume distribution for Λ

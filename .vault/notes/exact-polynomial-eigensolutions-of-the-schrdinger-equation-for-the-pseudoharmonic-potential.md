## Summary
This paper presents exact polynomial eigensolutions for the Schrödinger equation with the pseudoharmonic potential, applicable for any arbitrary angular momentum value. The authors Sameer M. Ikhdair and Ramazan Sever derive analytical solutions that extend the applicability of this molecular potential model. Published in the International Journal of Molecular Structure (THEOCHEM) in 2007.

## Key Facts
- The paper provides exact polynomial solutions to the Schrödinger equation for the pseudoharmonic potential
- Solutions are derived for any arbitrary angular momentum state
- Published in Int. J. of Molecular Structure (THEOCHEM) 806, 155 (2007)
- Authors: Sameer M. Ikhdair and Ramazan Sever
- Submitted November 2006, revised December 2006, 10 pages
